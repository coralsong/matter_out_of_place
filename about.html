matter out of place

about everything

"matter out of place" was a term initially inspired from Purity and Dangers by Mary Douglas. in the book, Douglas talks about something becoming "dirty" because it violates cognitive expectations of where things belong. but this dirty doesn't really mean that the objects are dirty, but just like how shoes that belong on your feet being placed on a dining table or food smeared on clothing feels wrong, this feeling of discomfort is what she describes as "dirty." on the big picture, these are caused by social norms and functional fixedness in objects.


while walking around new york, there were moments where i've noticed objects in questionable locations. starting by simple photographs i started growing a collection of objects out of place. who knew there'd be so much humorous scenes around our neighborhood. with these materials, i have combined my documentations into three different media: an interactive book, dynamic poster and this website. bringing you a similar experience as mine, i hope you have the joy of finding unexpected things while looking around.


i. where - placement game
"how accurately can you fill in the blanks?"
the goal of my collection is to emphasize the humor that comes from unexpected two categories colliding. having to focus on placing the object "perfectly" into where you think it belongs into, we tend to forget actually enjoy the content infront of us. please ignore your score and simple have fun!


ii. what - mosaic game
"can you guess the odd one out?"
all images are blurred out and you cannnot see what is infront of you. all you get is a magnifying glass with great amounts of zoom. there are actually no right answers to what you guess the objects are. what you think it is, is probably right.


iii. why - lights out
"how accurately can you fill in the blanks?""
very similar to the classic game "lights out," i took the game to contrast from my cutout images to the images with holes. the goal is, just like the original, to flip all tiles to the same side; whether its cutouts or holes.
